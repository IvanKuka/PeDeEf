# Feature Design Questions

Before designing a substantial feature, inspect the relevant modules and tests, then ask one concise batch of grounded questions for unresolved product decisions.

Cover inputs and page selection, expected output, overwrite behavior, undo/redo, cancel/failure handling, password protection, privacy, platform support and acceptance criteria when relevant. Avoid asking about details already settled by code or the task.

Wait for decisions that affect behavior before writing the design or implementing dependent changes. Record confirmed decisions and remaining uncertainties in the active feature dossier. Routine fixes that preserve established behavior need no separate interview.
