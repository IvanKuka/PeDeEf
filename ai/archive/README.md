# Historical Design Archive

`feature-proposals/` preserves the original 24 feature proposals previously held in ignored `docs/features/`, including their original index. They describe findings and ideas from 2026-10-15; many features have since been implemented. Their priorities, baseline and proposed behavior are historical, not current requirements or evidence of GUI availability.

For current behavior start with `STRUCTURE.md`, `ai/task-routing.md`, `ai/knowledge/`, `ai/specs/` and actual source/tests. Preserve the original proposal text when moving it here. New completed dossiers may be archived only after durable rules are incorporated into current documentation.
